# 🌟 THE LAST STAR

> A story-driven, third-person 3D fantasy adventure built in Unity 6 (Universal Render Pipeline).

---

## 📖 Story Overview

In the world of **Aethelgard**, memories, emotions, and promises were once preserved as stars in the night sky. When an ancient catastrophe begins extinguishing the stars, collective amnesia threatens to wipe out mortal civilization.

You awaken as **The Little Star**—a celestial spirit of light with no memory of its origin. Armed with the power of starlight, you embark on an emotional quest across six forgotten realms to restore the lost constellations, unveil the secrets of the tragic **Forgotten King**, and confront **The Star Eater**.

---

## 👥 Main Characters

1. **The Little Star** — The playable protagonist, a glowing celestial spirit wielding starlight magic.
2. **Luma** — A magical fox companion with a glowing lantern tail who tracks hidden paths.
3. **Orin** — An eccentric master clockmaker working to mend stalled time mechanisms.
4. **Mira** — Forest warden safeguarding the living memories of nature.
5. **Nox** — A mysterious shadow creature formed of celestial void dust.
6. **Pip** — A brass mechanical bird scout deployed to activate remote switches.
7. **The Keeper** — An ancient tree guardian harboring the history of the First Pact.
8. **The Forgotten King** — A remorseful monarch whose tragic grief gave birth to the Great Silence.
9. **The Star Eater** — The cosmic void entity consuming the stars.

---

## 🗺️ The Six Realms of Aethelgard

1. **Region 1: The Forgotten Village** (Tutorial, Light Pulse, Awakening)
2. **Region 2: The Forgotten Forest** (Nature puzzles, Luma's tracking, The Keeper)
3. **Region 3: The Clockwork City** (Mechanical puzzles, Orin's workshop, Pip companion)
4. **Region 4: The Sunken Memory** (Flooded temple ruins, light reflections, Nox encounter)
5. **Region 5: The Broken Sky** (Floating archipelago, light bridges, celestial updrafts)
6. **Region 6: The Starless Citadel** (Cosmic confrontation, Star Eater boss, 3 endings)

---

## 🛠️ Technical Specifications

* **Engine:** Unity 6 (6000.0.x)
* **Render Pipeline:** Universal Render Pipeline (URP)
* **Target Platform:** Windows PC (Gamepad & Keyboard/Mouse)
* **Architecture:** Modular C# ScriptableObject-driven systems, Event Bus decoupling.
